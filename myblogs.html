Bara Imambara

Explore the Heritage of the City of Nawabs

Bara Imambara

Location: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh

Best Time to Visit: October to March

Timings: 6:00 AM – 5:00 PM

Entry Fee: ₹50 (Approx.)

About

Bara Imambara is one of the most famous historical monuments in Lucknow. Built in 1784 by Nawab Asaf-ud-Daula, it is known for its unique architecture and the famous Bhool Bhulaiya.

Travel Tips

  • Visit early in the morning.
  • Carry a water bottle.
  • Wear comfortable shoes.
  • Hire a guide for Bhool Bhulaiya.
